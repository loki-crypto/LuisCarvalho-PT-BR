AI-Powered WhatsApp Assistant

Artificial intelligence handling business inquiries around the clock

// the problem

Local business owners were spending hours each day on WhatsApp answering the same questions — opening hours, pricing, product availability, directions. Meanwhile, higher-priority clients were left waiting.

// the solution

I built a system connecting WhatsApp Business to an AI trained on the business's specific information. It responds automatically, understands conversation context, books appointments, and only escalates to a human when the situation requires it.

Full management system for a recycling cooperative

// the problem

The cooperative ran everything on paper and slow-loading spreadsheets. Material intake, output, administrative reports — all manual, slow, and error-prone. No manager had a clear picture of what was happening at any given moment.

// the solution

Fully digitized operation. Management gained real visibility into the business, and time spent on paperwork dropped dramatically. Now serves as a model for other cooperatives in the region.
